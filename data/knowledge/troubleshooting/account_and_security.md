# Account Security, 2FA & Password Recovery Troubleshooting

**Document ID**: KB-TRB-001  
**Category**: troubleshooting  
**Version**: 1.9  
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-24  
**Authoritative Source**: Information Security & Identity Operations  

## 1. Password Reset & OTP Delivery Issues
- **OTP Delays**: SMS OTPs are valid for 10 minutes. If delayed due to telecom provider congestion, customers can select 'Verify via Email' or 'Verify via WhatsApp OTP'.
- **Account Lockout**: 5 consecutive incorrect password entries trigger a temporary 30-minute security lock. Automated unlock takes effect after 30 minutes, or instantly via password reset link.

## 2. Changing Registered Email & Mobile Number
- Changing account primary phone or email requires Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) verification via the currently registered contact methods.
- If the customer has lost access to both phone and email, account identity verification is conducted via Government ID proof submission through a Tier-2 Support Specialist.

## 3. Account Deletion & Data Privacy
- In accordance with enterprise data protection regulations, customers may request complete account data deletion under Settings > Privacy > Request Erasure.
- Active orders or pending refunds must be completed or settled prior to deletion processing.
