# Enterprise Priority Pass & Subscription Policy

**Document ID**: KB-SUB-001  
**Category**: subscription  
**Version**: 2.0  
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-19  
**Authoritative Source**: Customer Loyalty & Subscription Management  

## 1. Membership Benefits
- **Priority Dispatch**: Guaranteed same-day fulfillment on orders placed before 16:00 IST.
- **Zero Shipping Fees**: Unlimited free standard and express deliveries with no minimum cart value.
- **Extended Return Window**: 45 days return eligibility on all standard retail items (vs 30 days standard).
- **Dedicated Concierge**: Direct routing to Senior Support Leads with priority queue placement.

## 2. Renewal & Cancellation
- **Auto-Renewal**: Subscriptions renew automatically every 12 months. Renewal notification emails are dispatched 14 days prior to billing.
- **Cancellation**: Customers can cancel subscription anytime. Unutilized membership months are refunded on a pro-rata basis.
