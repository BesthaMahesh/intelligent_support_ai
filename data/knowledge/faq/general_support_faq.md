## Support Availability
- **AI Support Assistant**: Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for instant resolutions, order tracking, and policy inquiries.
- **Human Specialist Desk**: Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 20:00 IST.
- **Support Email**: enterprise-support@support.ai
- **Escalation SLA**: High-priority tickets are acknowledged within 1 hour by dedicated case managers.

## Order Tracking Process
Customers can track orders at any time by quoting their Order ID (format: ORD-XXXXX) in the chat window. The system retrieves real-time courier telemetry directly from logistics partners.
