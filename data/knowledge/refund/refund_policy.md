# Refund and Reimbursement Guidelines

**Document ID**: KB-REF-001  
**Category**: refund  
**Version**: 2.0  
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-18  
**Authoritative Source**: Finance & Support Governance  

## Refund Timelines by Payment Method
- **UPI Payments**: 24 to 48 business hours directly to the originating VPA.
- **Credit / Debit Cards**: 3 to 5 business days depending on bank billing cycles.
- **Net Banking**: 3 to 7 business days via IMPS/NEFT routing.
- **Store Credit / Wallet**: Instant (within 15 minutes of approval).

## Financial Safeguards & Authorization Limits
- **Automated AI Authorization**: Up to ₹10,000 for standard returns with verified courier pickups.
- **Supervisor Approval (> ₹10,000)**: Any refund claim exceeding ₹10,000 requires two-step verification and authorization by a Tier-2 Support Lead or Finance Manager.
- **Order Cancellation Refunds**: Initiated immediately upon cancellation confirmation before order dispatch.
