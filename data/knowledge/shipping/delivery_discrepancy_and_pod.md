# Delivery Discrepancy & Proof of Delivery (POD) Guidelines

**Document ID**: KB-SHP-002  
**Category**: shipping  
**Version**: 2.3  
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-28  
**Authoritative Source**: Logistics Quality Assurance Desk  

## Overview
This document outlines the standard enterprise operating procedure for investigating delivery status discrepancies, packages marked delivered but not received, and signature verification.

## 1. Package Marked "Delivered" but Not Received
If a customer reports that their order status indicates 'Delivered' but the package has not arrived:
1. **Immediate Carrier Geo-Location Verification**: The system checks carrier GPS coordinates captured at the moment of delivery scan against the customer's delivery destination.
2. **48-Hour Buffer & Neighborhood Check**: Customers are advised to verify with building security, front desk, or neighbors, as couriers may deposit packages in designated secure drop points.
3. **Escalation Trigger**: If the shipment remains unaccounted for 24 hours post-delivery scan, an automated 'Lost in Transit' investigation ticket is filed with carrier dispatch.

## 2. Address Modification Post-Order Placement
- **Before Dispatch**: Customers may change their shipping address directly within 2 hours of order placement or prior to warehouse picking.
- **After Dispatch (In Transit)**: Rerouting in transit is permitted only within the same delivery hub postal code. Inter-state rerouting requires order cancellation and reorder.

## 3. Damaged Outer Packaging & Open Delivery
- If the outer parcel seal is broken or tampered with at the time of courier handoff, customers are instructed to reject the delivery and request an OTP-verified carrier return.
- If accepted, photographic proof must be uploaded within 24 hours to initiate a priority replacement.
