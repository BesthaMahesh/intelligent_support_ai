# Shipping & Delivery Policy

**Document ID**: KB-SHP-001  
**Category**: shipping  
**Version**: 3.1  
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-20  
**Authoritative Source**: Global Logistics Division  

## Dispatch Timelines
- Standard Orders: Dispatched within 24 to 48 hours from the nearest regional fulfillment center.
- Express Priority Orders: Dispatched same-day if placed before 14:00 IST.
- Weekend Processing: Orders placed on Sundays or National Holidays are processed on the subsequent business day.

## Transit & Delivery SLAs
- Metro Cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 2 to 3 business days.
- Tier-2 & Tier-3 Cities: 4 to 6 business days.
- Remote / North-East Locations: 6 to 9 business days.

## Delayed Shipments
If an order has not shipped past its expected dispatch date:
1. Automated warehouse replenishment check is triggered.
2. If delays exceed 48 hours past dispatch date without courier tracking generation, the case is automatically prioritized for support escalation and courier allocation reassignment.

## Free Shipping Threshold
Free delivery is available on all orders exceeding ₹999. A flat delivery fee of ₹99 is applicable for orders below ₹999.
