# Invoicing, Tax Invoices & Corporate GST Billing

**Document ID**: KB-INV-001  
**Category**: invoice  
**Version**: 1.7  
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-22  
**Authoritative Source**: Finance & Compliance Department  

## 1. Obtaining Invoices
- Invoices are automatically generated and emailed in PDF format immediately upon order dispatch.
- Customers can re-download signed digital tax invoices anytime through the self-service portal or by requesting via the AI assistant.

## 2. Business GST Invoicing & Tax Input Credits
- Customers entering a valid GSTIN during checkout will receive an official B2B e-invoice with QR code and IRN (Invoice Reference Number) adhering to national GST mandates.
- **GST Invoicing Amendments**: Amendments to GSTIN or legal company name can only be requested prior to warehouse dispatch and invoice generation.

## 3. Invoice Discrepancies & Price Corrections
- If an invoice reflects incorrect item quantity or missing promotional discounts, the support desk will issue an amended credit note within 2 business days.
