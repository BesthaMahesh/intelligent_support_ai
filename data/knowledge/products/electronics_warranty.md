# Electronics Brand Warranty & Service Coverage

**Document ID**: KB-PROD-001  
**Category**: products  
**Version**: 1.2  
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-01  
**Authoritative Source**: Product Lifecycle Assurance  

## Warranty Duration
- Brand New Electronics (Laptops, Smartphones, Smartwatches): 1-Year Comprehensive Manufacturer Brand Warranty.
- Accessories (Cables, Chargers, Cases): 6 Months Limited Warranty.
- Audio Devices (Headphones, Soundbars): 1-Year Replacement Warranty for manufacturing defects.

## Claiming Warranty Service
Customers can visit authorized service centers across India with the original invoice or raise an assisted warranty pickup request through our support portal.
