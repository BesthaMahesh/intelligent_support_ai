# Apparel, Footwear & Home Goods Return Policy

**Document ID**: KB-RET-002  
**Category**: returns  
**Version**: 2.1  
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-18  
**Authoritative Source**: Merchandising & Returns Governance  

## 1. Apparel & Footwear Return Window
- **Standard Return Window**: 14 calendar days from delivery date.
- **Mandatory Requirements**: Items must be unused, unwashed, unaltered, and with all original brand tags, price tickets, hangers, and dust bags intact.
- **Footwear Special Condition**: Shoes and sneakers must be tried on carpeted surfaces. Soles showing scuff marks, dirt, or outdoor wear are non-returnable.

## 2. Innerwear & Personal Hygiene Exceptions
- For hygiene and health safety compliance, innerwear, socks, swimsuits, and personal grooming appliances cannot be returned once original plastic hygiene seals are broken.

## 3. Large Appliances & Furniture Return Window
- **Damaged on Arrival**: Inspection report must be filed within 48 hours of technician unboxing.
- **Trial Window**: 10-day replacement window for manufacturing defects.
- **Return Pickup Process**: Logistics team schedules doorstep technician pickup with heavy item packaging provided by the courier team.
