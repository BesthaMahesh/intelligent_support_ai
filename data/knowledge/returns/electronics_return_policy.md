# Electronics Return Policy

**Document ID**: KB-RET-001  
**Category**: returns  
**Version**: 2.4  
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-15  
**Authoritative Source**: Operations & Compliance Team  

## Overview
Electronics products can be returned within 30 days of delivery, provided they are unused and include the original packaging, serial number tags, accessories, and warranty cards.

## Eligible Return Conditions
1. **Defective or Damaged Products**: If an electronic item arrives dead-on-arrival (DOA) or physically damaged, customers must report the issue within 48 hours of delivery.
2. **Wrong Item Received**: If the customer received a model, color, or specification different from the order manifest.
3. **Packaging Requirements**: All original boxes, chargers, user manuals, and promotional freebies must be returned intact.

## Non-Returnable Scenarios
- Products with missing serial numbers or damaged IMEI tags.
- Software items, downloadable codes, or digital licenses once redeemed.
- Physical damage caused by customer misuse, water immersion, or unauthorized repair attempts.

## Replacement vs Refund
- Replacements are dispatched within 2 business days of the returned product undergoing technical warehouse inspection.
- If a replacement is unavailable in inventory, an automated full refund is credited to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
